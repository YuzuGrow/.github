  # YuzuGrow

  **Proven viral formats, recreated for your app. No creators needed.**

  YuzuGrow is an AI-powered UGC video platform for founders who need TikTok and
  Instagram video ads without hiring creators.

  ### How it works

  1. Browse a database of viral video formats proven to work
  2. Pick a format that fits your product
  3. Upload one photo to create your AI avatar
  4. Add your product demo
  5. Get a finished 9:16 video ad in minutes

  ### What makes it different

  - **Format-first**: Start from formats already proven to go viral, not blank
  scripts
  - **Your own face**: AI avatar from one photo, reusable across all videos
  - **Full pipeline**: Hook analysis, avatar gen, face swap, video creation,
  stitching, captions
  - **MCP Server**: 20 composable tools for Claude, Cursor, and ChatGPT
